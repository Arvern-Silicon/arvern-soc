# demo_raycaster — a walk through the aRVern maze

A first-person walk through a 16 × 16 maze in the style of the early first-person games,
rendered by the aRVern core and the Belenos graphic controller on the LT24: drawn at
160 × 120, 8 bpp, double-buffered, and shown at 320 × 240 by the refresh's pixel doubling.
Some walls carry the aRVern logo; aRVern logos float and spin in the corridors.

## Controls

- **Autopilot** by default: it walks from cell centre to cell centre, goes on straight or
  takes a side way at random (back only from a dead end) and turns on the spot.
- **SW[3]** walks forward, **SW[2]** turns left, **SW[1]** turns right; while any of them is
  on the autopilot is off, and it resumes from the nearest cell centre.
- **KEY[1]** turns around.
- The **LEDs** show the CPU load: the share of each frame spent drawing, one LED per eighth.

## How it is drawn

| Part | How |
|---|---|
| Walls | for each of the 160 columns a ray walks the map cell by cell (DDA, fixed point: the core has no FPU); its perpendicular distance gives the slice height, where it hit the texture column |
| Wall pixels | one GPU `EXEC_TEXSPAN` per column: a vertical run that steps down the texture column, the distance shade added to every texel (`SHADE`); the textures sit in the VRAM between the two frames |
| Floating logos | sorted far to near, clipped against the wall distances column by column; each visible column is one `EXEC_TEXSPAN` from the poster texture, its plate colour transparent |
| Ceiling and floor | 32 bands each of two lines, a palette ramp: one GPU gradient fill each (`blns_gpu_gradient`, the colour + 1 every 2 lines) of up to 64 lines from the horizon (which sways with the steps: head bob), the ramp's end colour filled on beyond them: 2–4 fills a frame instead of 64 |
| Shading | 6 distance shades, north-south walls one shade darker: all in the palette (32 texture colours × 6 shades), free per pixel |
| Animation | the circuit walls' traces use four colours along a diagonal pattern whose brightness rotates (light running along them); the posters' logo traces pulse; only palette entries change |
| Minimap | see-through: one GPU FILL with the palette remap `DST` (`GPU_REMAP_EN`) over its area turns every pixel of the view under it into the same colour three shades darker (a ceiling or floor band twelve bands towards its dark end), through a 256-byte table after the textures; its walls, the logos and the player are drawn over it. Without the remap it sits on a dark plate. Its frame is one GPU rectangle outline (`blns_gpu_rect_outline_xy`), and the view is drawn in GPU lines from the player, clipped to the map: the field of view's two edges dashed (2 pixels on, 2 off: the line pattern), five cells deep across the walls, and the heading solid, up to the wall ahead |
| Frames | drawn in the hidden frame, then a frame-pointer flip with the new palette (double-buffered LUT) |

## Walls

| Map | Wall | Texture |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | brick | 64 × 64, from `tex_data.h` |
| 2 | brick with an aRVern logo plate | 128 × 128, from `tex_data.h` |
| 3 | circuit board (traces, pads, a chip) | 64 × 64, generated at start-up into RAM |
| 4 | stone blocks | 64 × 64, generated at start-up |
| 5 | metal panels with rivets | 64 × 64, generated at start-up |

`tex_data.h` is generated by [`tools/tex2c.py`](tools/tex2c.py) from the logo SVGs (it
reuses the logo demo's converter) and is committed; `make textures [PREVIEW=file.png]`
regenerates it. The map is in `ray.c`. The sine table and three of the textures are
computed at start-up into RAM, then all the textures are copied into the VRAM, where the
GPU reads them.

## Build and run

```
make            # demo_raycaster.elf / .ihex
make run        # load over the JTAG DTM (see the Makefile for UART / I2C)
```

or build it into a bitstream: `synthesis/altera/0_create_bitstream.sh demo_raycaster`.

The board-level simulation (`-DSIMULATION`: 24 frames of autopilot, then the LEDs show
`0xA5`) saves the panel image after every frame as `frame_log/fb_<n>.png`:

```
cd ../../../sim/rtl_sim/run
SIMULATION_DEFINES="-D NO_TIMEOUT" ./run demo_raycaster -nodump
```
